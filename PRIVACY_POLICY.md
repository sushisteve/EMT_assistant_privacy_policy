# EMT Assistant Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [Nov. 10. 2026]

[] ("we", "us")Ndizi Inc. makes EMT Assistant, an Apple Watch and iPhone app that helps emergency medical technicians log call timestamps, scene locations, and voice notes. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

## The short version

EMT Assistant does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data. Everything you record stays on your own Apple Watch and iPhone. We have no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and we cannot see anything you record in the app.

## Information the app stores on your devices

When you use the app, it creates the following information and stores it **only on your Apple Watch and paired iPhone**:

- **Call logs:** the start and end times of each call and the timestamps of events you log (for example, Dispatch, En Route, On Scene, Transport, Clear).
- **Location:** a single GPS location captured when you log the "On Scene" event. The app does not track your location continuously or in the background.
- **Voice notes:** audio recordings you make by holding the log button on your Apple Watch.
- **Transcripts:** text transcriptions of your voice notes, created on your iPhone.

## How the app uses device permissions

| Permission | Device | Why it's used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location (While Using the App) | Apple Watch | To record the location of the scene when you log "On Scene". |
| Microphone | Apple Watch | To record voice notes during a call. |
| Speech Recognition | iPhone | To transcribe voice notes into text. |

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in Settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature won't work, but the rest of the app will continue to function.

## How your information moves

- **Watch to iPhone:** When you sync, call logs, locations, and voice notes are sent from your Apple Watch to your paired iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This is a direct connection between your own devices; the data is not sent to us or to any third party.
- **Transcription:** Voice notes are transcribed **on your iPhone** using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio is not sent to us or to any transcription server. The first time you transcribe, iOS may download a speech language model from Apple; this download does not include your recordings.
- **Maps:** The iPhone app displays scene locations on a map provided by Apple Maps. To draw the map, your iPhone requests map imagery from Apple for the area being displayed. Apple's handling of that data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Backups

Because your data is stored in the app on your devices, it may be included in your iPhone or Apple Watch backups (for example, iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

## Sensitive and patient information

Voice notes and transcripts may contain information about patients or incidents if you choose to record it. That information remains on your devices under your control. You are responsible for using the app in line with your employer's or agency's policies and any laws that apply to you, including patient privacy laws such as HIPAA. EMT Assistant is a personal logging tool and is not a substitute for official patient care reports.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your devices until you delete it.

- **On iPhone:** swipe left on a call in the Calls list, or use the delete option on a call's detail screen. Deleting a call removes its events, locations, voice notes, and transcripts from the iPhone.
- **On Apple Watch:** calls remain on the watch after they are synced. Deleting the app from your Apple Watch removes all of its data from the watch.
- **Everything:** deleting the app from both devices permanently removes all of its data, apart from any copies in backups you have made.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot access, recover, or delete it for you.

## Children

EMT Assistant is intended for emergency medical professionals and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
